Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy describes how Individual Entrepreneur Ivanov Mikhail, the provider of Meeting Scheduler, handles information when you use the Zoom integration in Alice AI for Business. You can contact us at adrfoxserbia@gmail.com.

Information used and why

When you ask Alice to schedule a Zoom meeting, the integration uses the meeting title, start date and time, time zone, and duration. Zoom uses those details to create the meeting in the Zoom account you authorize. The meeting details and the participant join link are returned to Alice so they can be displayed in your conversation. The integration uses the permissions you approve on Zoom's authorization screen.

Zoom and Alice AI for Business process data as part of this workflow. Their own policies describe how they handle your Zoom account and conversations. If you contact us for help, we use the information you send to investigate and respond to your request.

Storage, sharing and removal

Alice AI for Business connects directly to Zoom's official MCP service. The operator of Meeting Scheduler does not run a server or database for the integration and does not independently store Zoom access or refresh tokens, meeting details, or participant join links. Zoom and Alice AI for Business handle their own records, including Zoom meetings and Alice chat history, under their respective policies and settings. Revoking access in Zoom ends the integration's access to your account. You can manage existing meetings in Zoom and chat history in Alice separately.

Support emails are retained in our Gmail inbox. We do not apply a fixed retention period. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence.

Your choices and rights

You can revoke the integration's access in Zoom App Marketplace under Manage → Added Apps. To ask about your information or exercise applicable data rights, including access, correction, or deletion, contact adrfoxserbia@gmail.com.
