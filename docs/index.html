Documentation

Connect your Zoom account

  1. In Alice AI for Business, open Tasks and go to Plugins.
  2. Open Meeting Scheduler and start the Zoom connection.
  3. Sign in to the Zoom account you want to use, review the permissions, and approve the connection.
  4. Return to Alice and select Meeting Scheduler for your task.

You need access to the plugin in Alice and permission to authorize it in Zoom. If your Zoom administrator requires app approval, request approval before connecting.

Schedule a meeting

In Alice, ask: “Schedule a 30-minute Zoom meeting titled Project sync tomorrow at 15:00 Europe/Moscow.” Answer any follow-up questions. When the meeting has been created, the conversation displays the meeting information and participant join link. Check the details before sharing the link.

Disconnect the integration

  1. Sign in to Zoom App Marketplace using the account you connected.
  2. Go to Manage → Added Apps, find Meeting Scheduler, and select Remove.
  3. Disconnect the plugin in Alice as well if the connection is still visible there.

Revoking access prevents the integration from making further authorized requests. Manage previously created meetings in Zoom. See the Privacy Policy for how remaining data is handled.

Troubleshooting

If Zoom declines authorization, check which Zoom account you signed into and whether your administrator must approve the app. If the meeting time is wrong, retry with an explicit time zone after checking whether the meeting already exists in Zoom. If the issue persists, contact support.
